Website privacy

Your privacy and safety matter.

This page explains what happens when you use this website, follow an outside link, or contact Whispers electronically.

Last updated September 4, 2026

Information collected by this website

The current website does not include an online contact form, advertising, or analytics. Like most websites, the hosting server may create technical logs that include information such as an IP address, browser type, requested page, and date and time of access. These logs are used to operate and protect the website.

Calls, texts, and email

When you use a phone, text, or email link, your device and service provider may keep a record of that communication. Email and standard text messages are not guaranteed to be private or secure. If you are concerned that someone is monitoring your device or accounts, use a safer device if possible and contact an advocate before changing settings.

Outside services

Donation and newsletter links open services operated by Network for Good or its providers. Those services collect and handle information under their own privacy practices. The fonts used by this website are stored on the same hosting account and do not require a connection to Google Fonts.

Quick Exit and browser history

Quick Exit changes the current page to Google. It does not erase browser history, messages, downloads, network records, or activity visible through monitoring software. For more guidance, visit the Staying Safe page.

Questions

For questions about this website notice, call or text 712-441-0049 or email whispersoflhj@gmail.com. Do not include sensitive details in email if doing so may put you at risk.
