02 What we do

Long relationships,
not short programs

We’ve found that building long-term, safe relationships is the key to healing and success. We get to know each woman’s strengths, involve her in work that is fulfilling, and help her rebuild a foundation of self-esteem.

Walking alongside

Court hearings, HHS meetings, rental searches, benefit applications. You don’t sit through any of it alone.

Safe housing

A secure apartment at the Talitha Koum campus for up to 18 months — long enough for the fog of trauma to lift.

Skills for independence

Employment readiness, education and job training, financial literacy, parenting, and the practical steps — a license, a bank account of her own.

Leading, not just receiving

Residents lead morning devotions, cook the evening meal, help lead Sunday worship. We want them using their voices.

03 Talitha Koum Healing Campus

"Little girl, get up."

The words Jesus spoke in Mark 5:41, and the name of the campus we bought in 2023. Our first residents moved in September 2024. Two family apartments, six studios, a secure entry, and a community room. A family can stay up to 18 months, at no cost.
