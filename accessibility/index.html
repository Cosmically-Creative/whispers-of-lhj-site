Accessibility

Everyone should be able to find help and information.

Whispers of Love, Hope, and Joy is working to make this website usable for people with a wide range of abilities, devices, and assistive technologies.

Last updated September 4, 2026

Our approach

We aim to provide clear headings, keyboard-accessible navigation, meaningful link text, image descriptions, visible focus indicators, responsive layouts, and support for visitors who reduce motion. We continue to test and improve the website as content changes.

Need information another way?

If a page is difficult to use or you need information in another format, call or text 712-441-0049 or email whispersoflhj@gmail.com. Please identify the page or information you need and the format that would work best for you.

Outside services

The donation and newsletter pages are operated by an outside provider. Their accessibility may differ from this website. Contact Whispers if an outside service prevents you from completing an action and you need another option.

Safety-sensitive assistance

Calls, texts, and emails may leave records on a device or account. If you are concerned that someone is monitoring your activity, use a safer device if possible. For immediate danger, call 911. For 24/7 statewide support, call 1-800-770-1650 or text IOWAHELP to 20121.
